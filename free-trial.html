    Before you book

    Quick answers to the questions people ask most.

    Do I need any Spanish to start?

    No. Stage 1 starts from zero, so complete beginners are very welcome. If you already know some Spanish, take our free level test and we'll suggest the right stage.

    What happens in the free trial lesson?

    You join a lesson at the school, meet a teacher and experience the Callan Method for yourself. Your teacher also confirms the right stage for you. There's no obligation to sign up afterwards. length and format of the trial lesson

    When are the classes?

    Monday to Friday. In the mornings, lessons start at 10:30am and 11:45am; in the evenings, at 6:30pm and 7:45pm. Each lesson lasts one hour. weekend classes

    How do I get there?

    We're on Walworth Road, about a 10-minute walk from Elephant & Castle station (Northern and Bakerloo lines, plus National Rail). Plenty of bus routes stop along Walworth Road too.
