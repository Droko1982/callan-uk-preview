The Callan Method

Learn Spanish by speaking it, from the very first lesson

The Callan Method is a direct, speaking-first way to learn a language. Your teacher asks, you answer, and you're corrected as you go – so you get used to thinking in Spanish instead of translating.

Try the mini lessonBook a free trial lesson

How a lesson works

  1. The teacher asks, you answer

    Quick questions, each said twice. You answer straight away, in a full sentence – no time to translate in your head.

  2. You speak from the first minute

    Most of the lesson is you listening and speaking, not filling in worksheets in silence.

  3. Mistakes fixed on the spot

    Your teacher says the correct form and you try again straight away, so good habits form early.

  4. Revision built in

    Every lesson goes back over earlier material, so last week’s words are still there next week.

Inside a lesson

The same clear pattern, every time

Every lesson follows a predictable structure, so you always know what to expect – and you can see your progress from one week to the next.

  1. 1

    Revision

    You go back over questions and answers from earlier lessons. Repetition at the right moments is what makes new language stick.

  2. 2

    New work

    Your teacher introduces new words and grammar through questions, and you use them straight away in full sentences.

  3. 3

    Reading

    You read aloud from the course book while your teacher helps with pronunciation and corrects any slips.

Is it right for me?

An honest answer

The Callan Method suits many learners, but not everyone. Here's how to tell.

It's a great fit if…

  • You want to speak and understand, not just study grammar
  • You like a clear structure and knowing what comes next
  • You learn best by doing, and enjoy a brisk pace

You might prefer something else if…

  • You mainly want relaxed conversation practice
  • You are preparing for a specific exam

Not sure? A free trial lesson is the easiest way to find out.
