How a Callan lesson works

Less sitting and listening. More speaking.

The Callan Method keeps you talking. Short, fast-paced lessons follow the same clear pattern every time, so you always know where you are – and you can feel yourself improving.

Try a mini lesson

  1. The teacher asks, you answer

    Quick questions, each said twice. You answer straight away, in a full sentence – no time to translate in your head.

  2. You speak from the first minute

    Most of the lesson is you listening and speaking, not filling in worksheets in silence.

  3. Mistakes fixed on the spot

    Your teacher says the correct form and you try again straight away, so good habits form early.

  4. Revision built in

    Every lesson goes back over earlier material, so last week’s words are still there next week.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need any Spanish to start?

No. Stage 1 starts from zero, so complete beginners are very welcome. If you already know some Spanish, take our free level test and we'll suggest the right stage.

What happens in the free trial lesson?

You join a lesson at the school, meet a teacher and experience the Callan Method for yourself. Your teacher also confirms the right stage for you. There's no obligation to sign up afterwards. length and format of the trial lesson

Do you teach Spanish from Spain or from Latin America?

Spanish from Spain and from Latin America share almost all their grammar; the main differences are vocabulary, accent and a few forms such as vosotros, and good teaching prepares you for both. which variety the course follows and where the teachers are from

When are the classes?

Monday to Friday. In the mornings, lessons start at 10:30am and 11:45am; in the evenings, at 6:30pm and 7:45pm. Each lesson lasts one hour. weekend classes
